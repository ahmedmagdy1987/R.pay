## 2. Conclusions that shape Concept 08

### 2.1 Hero
1. **One object, one event, continuous presence.** The R.Pay terminal is the only hero object; the tap is the only hero event. The terminal never leaves the story from first paint to the demo CTA (Terminal Industries / Lando / Oryzo pattern).
2. **One kinetic sentence at first paint** — «لمسة واحدة. تحكّم كامل.» / "One Tap. Total Control." Nothing competes: no chip rows, no stat widgets, no secondary headline in the opening frame. (Every 2025–26 SOTD commits to one sentence + one focal object; the repo's own `latest` concept documents the opposite failure.)
3. **Object + consequence in one composition.** The tap is shown already producing its outcome — pulse, machine glow, and the story resolving toward the operator's view. Hardware alone is never the story (Stripe Terminal, Adyen).
4. **First paint is a finished poster still.** No preloader, no blank states; the film fades in behind already-rendered text (Apple poster-frame discipline + repo's proven `FilmHero` text-first LCP pattern).
5. **Chromatic commitment.** Near-monochrome blue-black base; **cyan** carried by exactly: the tap pulse, network lines, status LEDs. **Warm amber** carried by exactly: CTAs (+ the human-touch glow). Nothing else gets color (Jeton totality; flow.css accent-contract precedent).
6. **CTA visible at second one** — in the nav and in the hero — and the entire scroll resolves into it (Terminal Industries persistent DEMO + Oryzo CTA-as-punchline).

### 2.2 Motion
1. Four chapters + close, mapped to the buying decision: **Tap → Action → Network → Control → Demo.** One idea per viewport; if a movement doesn't prove a claim, it's cut (NEOM chapter rhythm).
2. **The motion budget is spent on the hero.** Below the fold: composed stills and short play-once, entry-triggered loops (Square's concentration; Apple's current default). No page-wide scrub, no scroll hijack.
3. Every animation **resolves into a composed still** that is the next section's layout (Jeton sticky-message, Apple resolve-to-still).
4. **RTL mirrors motion, not just layout.** Network flow and reveal directions are parameterized on `dir` (NEOM standard). This drove the choice of a live canvas scene over baked video for the network chapter.
5. Physics-weighted easing, calm pacing — "Total Control" is composed authority, not racing energy. One motion law: 400ms `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` (flow's proven system).
6. **The degraded state is art-directed**: reduced-motion / save-data / video-failure get designed poster sequences with full CTAs (Stripe BFCM's designed fallbacks).

### 2.3 CTA
1. One verb, verbatim, everywhere: **«احجز عرضًا مباشرًا» / "Book a live demo"** — 5 placements (nav, hero, post-network, post-proof, close), all resolving to the same destination (Nayax single-label model; Cantaloupe's 12-label sprawl is the measured failure).
2. Destination = **WhatsApp deep link** `wa.me/966550796555` with an Arabic prefill — in Saudi, WhatsApp is the friction remover; booking is two taps, zero typing (Foodics funnel evidence). Verified repo channel; no invented Calendly/forms/emails.
3. Supporting microcopy sells the conversation: «شاهد كيف تعمل R.Pay على ماكيناتك.» / "See how R.Pay works with your machines."
4. Proof sits immediately before the ask: the verified stats row and partner logos land right above the final CTA (belief peaks → ask lands).
5. Secondary hero action **stays on the page**: «شاهد عملية الدفع» / "Watch the payment flow" replays/advances the hero sequence.

### 2.4 Arabic
1. **Arabic is authored, not translated.** «لمسة واحدة. تحكّم كامل.» composes two verified fragments already in the repo (DropSequence beat + flow Act V heading) — it reads native because it is. AR/EN are true pairs everywhere (the `video-hero` concept's mismatched pair is the documented defect).
2. Arabic display type gets **larger optical size and tighter leading** than the Latin (Arabic runs ~20–25% shorter, no capitals).
3. Language toggle is a single equal-status header element, no flags (NEOM/Tamara/Foodics standard); the repo's `.ar-t`/`.en-t` + `html.en` system already implements this correctly.
4. HUD/caption labels inside the cinematic layer exist in both languages; LED/mono windows are **digits-only** (IBM Plex Mono has no Arabic — repo-documented constraint).

### 2.5 Anti-patterns (deliberately rejected)
- Apple pastiche (white-void studio look, "Get the highlights", superlative formulas) — borrow grammar, never skin.
- Template smells: three-icon benefit rows, logo carousel above the fold, equal-weight card grids, FAQ-as-architecture, glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text on every heading.
- Purple fintech gradients; neon everywhere; 7-layer glow stacks (the repo's `latest` hero documents the hierarchy collapse).
- Page-wide WebGL, walk-to-navigate, conversion-less chapters (Igloo-class awards pieces that convert nothing).
- External video embeds with player chrome; hot-linked CDN media (repo's `cinema` concept is the in-house cautionary tale — placeholder `__HERO_VIDEO_TALL__` shipped).
- Invented stats, fake testimonials, invented certifications (SAMA/PCI appear nowhere in the repo → they do not appear in 08).
- Baked-in headlines inside generated media; AI text artifacts; unstable logos.
- Multiple competing CTAs; CTA label drift.
